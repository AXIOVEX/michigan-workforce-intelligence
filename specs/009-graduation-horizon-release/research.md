# Research decisions and limitations

Review cutoff: September 26, 2026 America/Detroit; capture spans September 26–27 UTC. Latest published periods differ by indicator. Sources and individual observations are in the edition register; 187 records include explicit unavailable observations, not 187 independent signals. Thirty-six selected projection rows cover 18 occupations in two different vintages.

Official government statistics can stand alone; seek corroboration without treating republication of the same BLS series as independent. Do not call sources certified without an explicit certification. University of Michigan sentiment is national. Chicago Fed district activity is regional context, not a Michigan estimator. NY Fed graduate underemployment is not U-6. Michigan U-6 has an unusual 11-month window because October 2025 is absent.

Resolved challenges: use final September consumer sentiment 48.1 rather than cached preliminary 47.8; use identified Chicago Fed table/PDF instead of conflicting summary prose; do not compare state seasonally adjusted and county unadjusted rates; the regional projection workbook is 2022–32 while the state workbook is 2024–34. County August BLS API values were unavailable. Empty Socrata objects are not usable observations.

University decisions are conditional governance proposals, not institution-specific capacity determinations. Local placement, program cost, completions-to-openings alignment, verified employer vacancies and student debt are missing. No defensible occupation extinction dates were found. Radiology is a curriculum redesign case alongside software, bookkeeping/accounting, legal support, design, administration, customer service and manufacturing. Physician/technologist scope and licensure remain separate.

Raw artifacts are hashed; public BLS originals are packaged, while copyrighted full pages stay outside the public archive. Evidence integrity establishes traceability, not causal truth or future employment.
